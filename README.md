## 🌿 The Creative Concept: "The Horizon Viewfinder"

Inspired by the **SmartDuke Studio** motion templates (**Petrel's** cut-paper SVG layered parallax, **Cirrus's** golden-hour scrub, and **Meridian's** interactive journey pass), this website was built from the ground up to embody a **woman-centric** visual philosophy:

### 1. The Interactive Woman Traveler & Smartphone Viewfinder (Centerpiece)
- **The Woman & Her Viewfinder**: An elegant silhouette and perspective-tilted smartphone HUD in the hero section.
- **Interactive Live Viewfinder**: Inside her phone screen, visitors experience:
  - Real-time weather and altitude dispatch (`Meghalaya • 19°C • Misty Morning`).
  - Interactive Camera Shutter: clicking the shutter triggers a tactile camera flash and saves the memory to the visitor's moodboard.
  - Live soundscape player: synthesizes serene nature winds, streams, and alpine chimes with the browser's Web Audio API without downloading heavy audio files.
  - One-tap WhatsApp deep-link to Founder Pallavi (+91 79999 19170).

### 2. Shifting Cut-Paper Parallax Scenarios
- Behind the traveler, layered vector cut-paper silhouettes (with multi-stop depth shadows and atmospheric sun discs) transition dynamically as you switch between dream destinations:
  - **Meghalaya**: Living Root Bridges, Nohkalikai waterfalls, and misty rainforest ridges.
  - **Royal Rajasthan**: Sandstone ramparts, Jharokha fort balconies, and sunset golden dunes.
  - **Kashmir**: Alpine Pir Panjal peaks, cedarwood houseboats, and Dal Lake shikara waters.
  - **Bali**: Stepped Tegallalang rice terraces, sacred bamboo retreats, and coastal ocean dusk.
  - **Vietnam**: Towering Halong Bay karst peaks and ancient Hoi An glowing silk lanterns.

### 3. Lightweight 60 FPS Petal & Fern Canvas Physics
- An organic HTML5 Canvas particle system animates delicate jasmine petals, gold dust, and fresh green fern fronds (the Fernway emblem motif: *"a fresh leaf reaching toward the sky"*).
- **Interactive Breeze**: Petals react to mouse movements, swirling gently away on hover. Visitors can also toggle wind speed (Calm, Breeze, Bloom).
- **Zero Bloat**: 100% vector SVG and canvas code. Zero multi-megabyte video files. **Total initial JavaScript payload is only ~36.9 kB!**
